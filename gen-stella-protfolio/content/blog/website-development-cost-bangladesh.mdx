## What Drives the Cost?

### 1. Number of pages and features
A simple 5-page brochure site is very different from a 50-page e-commerce platform with a payment gateway, inventory system, and admin panel. Every additional feature adds design time, development time, and testing effort.

### 2. Design complexity
Are you using a pre-built template, a custom design from a Figma file, or a full design-system-driven interface? Custom UI/UX design can add ৳15,000–50,000 to any project.

### 3. Technology stack
WordPress with a free theme costs almost nothing to set up. A Next.js application with TypeScript, Prisma, and PostgreSQL takes significantly more engineering skill. The technology is worth it — better performance, SEO, and scalability — but the price reflects that.

### 4. Integrations
Payment gateways (SSLCommerz, Stripe), CRM systems, Google Analytics, email marketing, third-party APIs — each one requires custom integration work.

### 5. Hosting and maintenance
Building the site is one-time cost. Hosting, SSL certificates, domain renewal, security updates, and content changes are ongoing. Budget ৳5,000–20,000/month for a properly maintained site.

## Red Flags: Why Cheap Quotes Are Risky

We regularly hear from business owners who paid ৳10,000 for a website that:
- Doesn't work on mobile
- Takes 8+ seconds to load
- Has no SSL certificate
- Can't be edited without the original developer

The cheapest quote is rarely the best value. Ask any potential developer:
1. Can I see 3 live examples of your work?
2. What happens if I need changes after delivery?
3. Is the code mine or hosted on your account?
4. How do you handle SEO?

## How to Get the Best Value

1. **Start with your goals** — What action should visitors take? That determines the design.
2. **Prioritise mobile** — 70%+ of traffic in Bangladesh is mobile. Mobile-first design is non-negotiable.
3. **Invest in content** — A well-written page outperforms a beautifully designed one with placeholder text.
4. **Budget for maintenance** — A website left unmaintained for 12 months often costs more to fix than to rebuild.
